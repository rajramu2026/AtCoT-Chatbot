About Atlantic Coast Tours

We're a small, local outfit running guided cliff walks, kayak trips, boat tours, cycling routes and food tours across Co. Clare, Co. Galway and Co. Mayo. Every guide is local, every group is small, and every booking supports the coastal communities we operate in. Availability, capacity and prices change week to week depending on weather, season and demand — so our assistant below always checks the live sheet before answering, rather than relying on a fixed script.
